GFR: kidney function calculator

Estimates glomerular filtration rate from creatinine and the inputs required by each equation. Includes CKD-EPI 2021, CKiD U25 and Schwartz, with method selection according to age. References and limitations for each estimate are available in the app.

Open GFR ↗

TI-RADS: thyroid nodules

Organises thyroid nodule ultrasound findings using ACR TI-RADS. Review the score, category and guidance related to the measurements and context entered, and prepare a description for professional review.

Open TI-RADS ↗

HepFe: liver iron by MRI

Analyses signal decay in MRI measurements to estimate liver iron. Provides curve fitting, calibration selection and export of a plot and text for review. Interpretation depends on the acquisition, the chosen model and its limitations.

Open HepFe* ↗

O-RADS: ovarian and adnexal lesions

Helps organise descriptors for an adnexal lesion on ultrasound or MRI using ACR O-RADS. The app presents the category, references and draft report text for review, with attention to the imaging modality and applicable clinical context.

Open O-RADS Calc ↗

N-Lung: pulmonary nodules on CT

Separates incidental nodule assessment using Fleischner recommendations from screening assessment using Lung-RADS. Brings together measurements, examination comparison and draft report text for review. Check the eligibility criteria before using each system.

Open N-Lung Calc ↗

Frequently asked questions

Before you
get started.

The essentials of access, availability and responsible use.

Who are the apps for?

They are intended for radiologists and other healthcare professionals familiar with each tool’s purpose and limitations. They are not a patient diagnostic service.

Do I need to install anything?

The five featured apps are accessible in a browser. Links open their original pages on alvaro-menezes.com in a new tab.

Are they on the App Store and Google Play?

This site presents the iOS, Android and web development initiative. Currently, the published links lead to web versions. Official store links will be added when releases are available.

Can I send examinations or patient data?

The RadApps website has no upload feature, medical record system or clinical form. Do not send identifiable patient data through the contact channel. Read each app’s own policy before entering information.

How do I report a problem or suggest an improvement?

Email admin@alvaro-menezes.com with the app, device, browser and steps to reproduce the issue, without patient data.
