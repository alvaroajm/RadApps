1. Our commitment

We aim to make this site accessible across devices and ways of navigating. This statement describes implemented features and is not a certification of full conformance.

2. Available features

  • Keyboard navigation, visible focus and a skip-to-content link.
  • Structured headings, image descriptions and labelled controls.
  • Portuguese and English versions with document language declared.
  • Light and dark themes, responsive layout and respect for reduced-motion preferences.
  • Content pages readable without JavaScript; theme switching and the compact menu depend on it.

3. Limitations and improvements

Original brand artwork includes English text embedded in images; alternative descriptions identify the brand and message. Linked apps have their own interfaces and are not covered by this statement. Compatibility may vary between browsers and assistive technologies.

4. Report a barrier

If you encounter a difficulty, contact admin@alvaro-menezes.com. Include the page, device, browser and, if you wish, the assistive technology used. Do not include patient data.
