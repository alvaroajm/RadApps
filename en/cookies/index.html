1. How this site works

RadApps uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for visit and performance statistics, without audience analytics cookies, according to the service documentation. The site does not set advertising cookies. There is no marketing consent banner because the site does not implement that processing. Infrastructure services may process technical information or use mechanisms necessary for security, according to their configuration and policies.

2. Light or dark theme

When you choose a theme, your browser stores the radapps-theme key in localStorage, with the value light or dark. This contains no visitor identifier, is not sent by our code to a server and remains until removed. If you do not choose a theme, the site follows your device setting.

3. Language

English pages use the /en/ path. Navigation preserves your chosen language through links, without a cookie or personal profile.

4. Manage your preference

You can remove the appearance preference with the button below or delete radapps.app data in your browser settings. This does not erase information from other websites or linked apps.

5. Changes and reference

If optional tracking features are introduced in the future, this policy and appropriate controls must be updated before activation. See also the Privacy policy. ANPD — Cookies e proteção de dados pessoais ↗.
